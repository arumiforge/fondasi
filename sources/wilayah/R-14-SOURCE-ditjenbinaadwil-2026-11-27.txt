WHAT THE ATTACHMENT COVERS (KESATU, page 4)
-------------------------------------------
This is a partial amendment. It replaces only these parts of the attachment to
Kepmendagri 300.2.2-2138 Tahun 2025, for Aceh and Sumatera Utara:
 1. Rekapitulasi Kode, Data Wilayah Administrasi Pemerintahan dan Pulau per Provinsi
    (huruf A, No. 1 Aceh and No. 2 Sumatera Utara)
 2. Rekapitulasi Jumlah Pulau per Provinsi (huruf D.a, No. 1 Aceh and No. 2 Sumatera Utara)
 3. Rekapitulasi Jumlah Pulau per Kab/Kota (D.b.1, Provinsi Aceh, No. 6 Kabupaten Aceh Singkil)
 4. Rincian Kode dan Data Pulau per Kab/Kota (D.c.1, Provinsi Aceh)
 5. Rekapitulasi Jumlah Pulau per Kab/Kota (D.b.2, Provinsi Sumatera Utara,
    No. 1 Kabupaten Tapanuli Tengah)
 6. Rincian Kode dan Data Pulau per Kab/Kota (D.c.2, Provinsi Sumatera Utara)
According to the preamble, the reason is the agreement between Aceh and Sumatera Utara of 17 June 2025
(No. 04/MOU/2025, No. 100.2.3.3/5270/2025). It places Pulau Mangkir Gadang, Pulau Mangkir Ketek,
Pulau Lipan and Pulau Panjang in Kabupaten Aceh Singkil, Aceh.
Decree 300.2.2-2138 Tahun 2025 remains in force where this decree does not change it (KEDUA a).
So the complete national tables for all other provinces are in the 2138 attachment, which this folder does not include.

1. JDIH not reachable. jdih.kemendagri.go.id did not load in the browser on three tries

   The changes are stated in the decree itself (KESATU, summarised above).
4. On the Bina Adwil site, the summary field ("Tentang") on the list and detail pages reads
   "Pemberian dan Pemutakhiran Kode, Data Wilayah Administrasi Pemerintahan, dan Pulau" and
   leaves out "Perubahan atas Keputusan Menteri Dalam Negeri Nomor 300.2.2-2138 Tahun 2025".
   The site's banner/notice text also describes 2430 as amending "Nomor 300.2.2-2430 2025"
   (itself), which is a typo. The printed document is authoritative (see above).
5. The number is printed with spaces around the dash ("300.2.2 – 2430"). Elsewhere, including the
   attachment header and the site, it appears as "300.2.2-2430".
