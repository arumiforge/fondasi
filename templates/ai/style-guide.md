# Visual identity and style guide — <APP>

1. **Identity**: audience, culture, values the visuals carry (e.g. Indonesian school athletes; santri and NU for SPMB).
2. **Respect rules** (strict, win over briefs): religious elements, clothing, gender, age, places; what never to show.
3. **Colour**: brand colour from settings (CSS variables); painting palette; dark variants if the app has dark mode.
4. **Paintings**: look, cast (same people across images), sizes and budgets, variants.
5. **Vectors (SVG)**: masks in `#000` painted by CSS variables, `currentColor` for buttons/loaders, no scripts.
6. **Never** (Fondasi `docs/ui.md`): AI-slop looks — gradient blobs, glassmorphism, floating 3D shapes, text in images.
